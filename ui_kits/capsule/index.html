02 · Variance partition

Where does the noise live?

A pelvic shape ratio R was measured six times on each of 29 lateral radiographs. Variance was partitioned across signal, session drift, and random error — opacity ∝ confidence, thickness ∝ component magnitude.

Read the partition

Of total variance, ~42% is signal, ~0% session drift, ~58% random error. FH landmarks are roughly 2× noisier than S1 corners. The stability subset (SD < 0.20, n=10) reaches ICCavg-6 = 0.97 — pixel-exact tooling matters most on patients near the population median where R changes slowly.
